Architects shape our future – yet the knowledge it takes is scattered across guidelines,
state building codes, project files and the experience of your own office.

Piloti connects project data, building law, budget and office knowledge in one place. In
this blog we share what we learn along the way.

## What to expect

The blog has two strands. In the [Journal](/en/blog/journal/) we write for offices:

- **Building law, explained** – we put OIB guidelines and state building codes into
  context and explain them.
- **From practice** – how planning firms put AI to good use today without giving up
  control.

In the [build log](/en/blog/bautagebuch/) we record how Piloti is built and why: how an
answer becomes traceable to its source, and what we learn from mistakes.

Want to try Piloti in your own office? [Become a pilot office](/en/#kontakt).
